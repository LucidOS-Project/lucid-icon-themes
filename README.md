# LucidOS Icon Themes
Light and dark mode icon themes for LucidOS.

A Note on Licensing & Intent:
I know that using a Non-Commercial (CC-BY-NC-SA 4.0) license for icons can feel a bit different from the standard GPLv3 expectations in the Linux community. However, I’ve put a months of work  into perfecting the icons in my vision, so my goal is simple: I want to keep these icons free for the community while preventing others from selling $5 "copies" or profiting directly from my art.

Content Creators & Collabarators: Despite the "NC" tag, I give full permission to anyone featuring LucidOS in videos, blogs, reviews, or products even if your content is monetized. Your support helps the project grow!
